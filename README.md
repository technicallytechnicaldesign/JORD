A slightly chaotic world companion with chaotic things? Theres a garden, a kid mode, a shelf for collecing stuff. 
There are little exersizes and you can track stuff about your vibe throughout the day. Its chill. 
Basically just a little experiment in creating the shit you want to use for self-reflection, radical acceptance and being present without having to sign up for a service or give data away. 
It runs in your browser, and nothing gets sent anywhere. So you do you and poke the little round cutie and collect spiders and shit for fun. No stress.
